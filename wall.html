Who We Are

About DeLiverance Church Illasit

DeLiverance Church Illasit is a place where people come together to worship God, grow spiritually and experience the love of Jesus Christ.

We believe in the power of prayer, the authority of God's Word, Christian fellowship and sharing the Gospel with our community.

Our doors are open to everyone. Whether you are beginning your faith journey or looking for a church family, you are welcome.
